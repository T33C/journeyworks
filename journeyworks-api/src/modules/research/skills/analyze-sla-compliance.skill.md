# Analyze SLA Compliance

## Description

Track SLA breach rates — how many cases met or missed their service-level targets.

## When to Use

- User asks about SLA compliance or breach rates
- "What's our SLA breach rate?", "How many cases missed their SLA targets?", "Show me SLA compliance..."
- Looking for SLA-related metrics

## Examples

- "What's our SLA breach rate?"
- "How many cases missed their SLA targets this month?"
- "Show me SLA compliance by category"
