# Analyze Customer Health

## Description

Get a health score for a specific customer with sentiment trends, risk factors, and actionable recommendations.

## When to Use

- User asks about a customer's health score or relationship status
- "What's the health score for...", "Is this customer at risk of churning?", "Give me a health assessment..."
- Looking for risk factors and actionable recommendations for a specific customer

## Examples

- "What's the health score for customer C-1042?"
- "Is this customer at risk of churning?"
- "Give me a health assessment for Acme Corp"
