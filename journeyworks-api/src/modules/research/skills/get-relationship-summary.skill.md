# Get Relationship Summary

## Description

Comprehensive relationship overview for a customer — communication history, sentiment trajectory, key interactions, and active issues.

## When to Use

- User asks for a full relationship overview for a customer
- "Summarise our relationship with...", "Give me a full overview of interactions with...", "How has our relationship with this customer evolved?"
- Looking for a comprehensive view of a customer relationship

## Examples

- "Summarise our relationship with customer C-1042"
- "Give me a full overview of interactions with Acme Corp"
- "How has our relationship with this customer evolved?"
