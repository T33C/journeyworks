# Analyze Topics

## Description

Discover what topics and themes customers are discussing, with frequency and trend data.

## When to Use

- User asks what topics or themes customers are discussing
- "What are the top topics...", "What themes are emerging...", "What are VIP customers discussing?"
- Looking for trending subjects or frequent discussion points

## Examples

- "What are the top topics customers are talking about?"
- "What themes are emerging in recent complaints?"
- "What are VIP customers discussing?"
